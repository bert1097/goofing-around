2 Minute Drill

Real end-of-game drives. The coaches lost every one. Take the headset.

Pick a drive from NFL history. You get the same score, clock, field position and timeouts the coach had. Call every play, manage the clock, and see if you can change the ending.

Situations are reconstructed from public game accounts; timeouts and exact seconds are close approximations. Plays resolve with a simple probability model, so a great call can still fail and a bad one can still work.
